Haptics test

1. Toggle this switch with your finger. On iOS 18 or later you should feel a tick. If you feel nothing, haptics are off on the phone (Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics) and no web page can make them.

2. The app's method (v0.7.2+): a fresh hidden switch per tick. If 1 ticks but these don't, tell us.

3. The old method (up to v0.7.1): one hidden switch kept off-screen. For comparison only.
